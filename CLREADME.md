# Subtitle Studio: Staff Notes

Cobblestone Learning · *Learning. Creativity. Trust.*

A subtitle editor that runs in your web browser. Open a subtitle file (`.vtt` or `.srt`), play the video alongside it, and fix the wording and timing until the captions line up. It can also translate subtitles into another language. There is nothing to install, and your files stay on your own computer.

## What you can do with it
- Edit caption text and start and end times, add, delete, split and merge captions, and undo mistakes.
- Watch the video with the captions showing, to check they appear at the right moment.
- Move all the captions, or just the ones you pick, earlier or later in one go.
- Re-time captions when a video has been sped up or slowed down.
- Find and replace words across the whole file.
- Translate the captions, including into Irish, and check them before keeping the translation.

## How to use it
1. Open the live app (the link is at the top of the README), or open the `index.html` file on your computer.
2. Load your subtitle file, and the video if you want to watch along.
3. Make your changes, then use **Export** to download the finished file as `.vtt` or `.srt`.

## Good to know
- Translation uses an online translation service of your choice. You paste in your own key for it; the key is not saved to GitHub and is only sent to that service.
- Your subtitles and video are only sent anywhere when you choose to translate.
- This repo is public, so anyone can see it. Keep client files and keys out of it.
